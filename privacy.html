Privacy Policy

Game Bot and gamebotstudio.com · Last updated: November 13, 2026

Draft, pending legal review. This is a draft in plain English for public review. It has not been signed off by a lawyer and is not legal advice. Please have a lawyer review it before taking payment or treating it as final.

This policy describes how AlphaMax Studios (“we”, “us”) handles information for the Game Bot desktop app and this website. Questions: alphamaxstudios@gmail.com.

1. The app runs on your PC

Game Bot is a local desktop app. The AI models run on your computer. Your chats, projects, game files, and settings stay on your PC unless you choose to send them to us.

2. This website

This website (gamebotstudio.com) is a static site. It does not show ads, and we do not sell your data.

The site currently uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. That tool is provided by Cloudflare and is not used to show ads. Ordinary web-server logs (such as IP address, browser type, and time of visit) may also be kept by the host that serves the site (GitHub Pages) and by Cloudflare if your connection goes through them.

If you email us to join the waitlist or ask a question, we receive whatever you put in that email, such as your email address and optional details like your GPU. We use that only to reply and to notify you about Game Bot.

3. Update checks

When Game Bot checks for updates, it contacts gamebotstudio.com (by default https://gamebotstudio.com/gamebot/version.json) to read version information. That request can include ordinary web-server data such as your IP address and the time of the request. The update check is for version information and, when an update is published, a download link and a file checksum. It does not send your chats or projects.

4. License checks

If a license server is configured, Game Bot may contact that server to activate or periodically validate your license. That request sends:

  • your license key
  • a hashed machine ID (a fingerprint derived from this PC, not your name)
  • the app version

It does not send your chats, projects, or game files. If no license server is configured, the license can be bound on this PC without that online check.

5. Bug reports

Bug reports are sent only when you choose to send one. The usual path is that Game Bot builds a zip and opens an email draft; you review it, attach the zip if needed, and press send. A report can include the full chat, a game summary, logs, and system information such as the app version and hardware details. File paths in those logs may include your Windows username. We use bug reports only to fix problems and improve Game Bot.

An optional setting can send a report directly by email (SMTP). That option is off unless you configure it.

6. No ads in the app

The Game Bot app does not include ads, does not use advertising trackers, and does not sell your data. We do not use your chats or projects to train public cloud models, because the AI runs on your PC.

7. Children

Game Bot is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

8. Retention and deletion

Waitlist emails and bug reports are kept while we need them to notify you or to fix issues. To ask us to delete what you sent, email alphamaxstudios@gmail.com.

9. Changes

We will post updates to this policy at gamebotstudio.com/privacy.html.
