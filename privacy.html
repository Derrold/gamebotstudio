Privacy Policy

GameDevelopmentBot and gamedevelopmentbot.com · Effective November 17, 2026

This policy describes how AlphaMax Studios (“we”, “us”) handles information for the GameDevelopmentBot desktop app and this website. Questions: alphamaxstudios@gmail.com.

1. The app runs on your PC

GameDevelopmentBot is a local desktop app. The AI models run on your computer. Your chats, projects, game files, and settings stay on your PC unless you choose to send them to us.

2. This website

This website (gamedevelopmentbot.com) is a static site. It does not show ads, and we do not sell your data.

The site currently uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. That tool is provided by Cloudflare and is not used to show ads. Ordinary web-server logs (such as IP address, browser type, and time of visit) may also be kept by the host that serves the site (GitHub Pages) and by Cloudflare if your connection goes through them.

When you buy GameDevelopmentBot, checkout and payment are handled by Stripe; we do not see or store your card number. Stripe sends us your email address and order details so we can issue your license key and download. If you email us a question, we receive whatever you put in that email and use it only to reply.

3. Update checks

When GameDevelopmentBot checks for updates, it contacts gamebotstudio.com (by default https://gamebotstudio.com/gamebot/version.json) to read version information. That request can include ordinary web-server data such as your IP address and the time of the request. The update check is for version information and, when an update is published, a download link and a file checksum. It does not send your chats or projects.

4. License checks

If a license server is configured, GameDevelopmentBot may contact that server to activate or periodically validate your license. That request sends:

  • your license key
  • a hashed machine ID (a fingerprint derived from this PC, not your name)
  • the app version

It does not send your chats, projects, or game files. If no license server is configured, the license can be bound on this PC without that online check.

5. Bug reports

Bug reports are sent only when you choose to send one. The usual path is that GameDevelopmentBot builds a zip and opens an email draft; you review it, attach the zip if needed, and press send. A report can include the full chat, a game summary, logs, and system information such as the app version and hardware details. File paths in those logs may include your Windows username. We use bug reports only to fix problems and improve GameDevelopmentBot.

An optional setting can send a report directly by email (SMTP). That option is off unless you configure it.

6. No ads in the app

The GameDevelopmentBot app does not include ads, does not use advertising trackers, and does not sell your data. We do not use your chats or projects to train public cloud models, because the AI runs on your PC.

7. Children

GameDevelopmentBot is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Chats and projects stay on the local PC. The only data the app sends by choice is optional bug reports the user chooses to send, as described in section 5. Update checks, license checks, and this website’s visit analytics (Cloudflare Web Analytics and ordinary host logs) work as described above. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email alphamaxstudios@gmail.com and we will delete it.

8. Retention and deletion

Order records (email, license key and order details) are kept while your license is active and as needed for tax, accounting and dispute records. Bug reports are kept while we need them to fix issues. To ask us to delete what you sent, email alphamaxstudios@gmail.com.

9. Changes

We will post updates to this policy at gamedevelopmentbot.com/privacy.html.
